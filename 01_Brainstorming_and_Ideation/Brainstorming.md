# Brainstorming and Ideation

## Project Name
LegalEase – AI-Powered Legal Document Generator

## Project Idea
LegalEase is an AI-powered web application designed to help users generate structured legal document drafts based on the information provided by the user.

## Problem Identified
Creating legal documents manually can be time-consuming and difficult for users who are not familiar with legal document formats and clauses.

## Proposed Solution
The proposed system uses Artificial Intelligence to generate legal document drafts based on user-provided information.

## Initial Document Types
- Rental Agreement
- Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Legal Notice

## Future Ideas
- Tamil language support
- Multiple legal document templates
- PDF download
- Document history
- User login
- AI-assisted document editing

## Expected Outcome
The system should allow users to select a document type, enter the required details, generate an AI-assisted legal document draft, review it, and download it as a PDF.

## Important Note
The generated documents are drafts for informational and drafting assistance purposes and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before use.
